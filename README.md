# Disaster Management System

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Disaster Management System** is a database-driven application designed to manage disaster-related activities efficiently. The system helps organizations coordinate disaster response operations by managing shelters, victims, donations, volunteers, inventory, and relief distribution.

The main goal of this project is to provide a centralized platform where disaster information, resources, and support activities can be managed effectively.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- Manage disaster information in a structured database system
- Maintain records of affected people and emergency requests
- Manage shelters and available resources
- Track donations and relief distribution
- Manage volunteers and their activities
- Improve coordination during disaster response operations
- Reduce manual data management complexity

---

## ✨ Features

### 🌪 Disaster Management
- Add and manage disaster information
- Track disaster types, locations, and affected areas
- Maintain disaster records

### 🏠 Shelter Management
- Store shelter details
- Track shelter capacity and availability
- Manage occupied and available spaces

### 👥 Victim & Relief Request Management
- Maintain victim information
- Create relief requests
- Track request status

### 📦 Inventory Management
- Manage available relief items
- Track stock quantity
- Monitor distribution of resources

### 💰 Donation Management
- Store donor information
- Track donations
- Maintain donation history

### 🙋 Volunteer Management
- Register volunteers
- Manage volunteer information
- Assign volunteers to disaster activities

### 🚚 Relief Distribution
- Manage relief distribution records
- Track distributed resources
